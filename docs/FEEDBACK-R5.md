# Round 5: the owner's first real iPhone play test (iPhone 18 Pro, home-screen app)

His screenshots are in `docs/playtest-r5/` (look at them). His notes, in his words where quoted, with the Lead's decision and owner for each.
House rule he restated: **"a basic rule for building these games is avoid scrolling and words and condense with images/symbols to simplify."**

| # | Feedback | Lead decision | Owner(s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "A way to tell when you're buying animals if you already have one of that gender or have one of that gender but this one has better stats." | Every animal offered for sale (trader, director, market, crate reveal) shows, at a glance and without words: whether you already own one of that species and sex, and if so whether this one is better. Engine gives a lookup; screens show it as a small badge (for example a "have it" mark, an up arrow when this one beats your best of that sex, a down arrow when it is worse). | Rules (lookup), Menus + Moments (badge) |
| 2 | "There is overlapping words on the projects screen and also this screen should start at the top and running after." | Projects: the Start boxes come first, Running projects below. Fix the overlapping sub-labels under the Start boxes ("Convoy is out", "None ready" collide with the next row; screenshots 1 and 7). No scrolling. | Menus |
| 3 | "Purple symbol idk what that is it needs to be more self explanatory." | The purple microphone (Hosting skill) in the "A big idea" choice (screenshot 6) is unreadable. Redraw it as an unmistakable stage microphone, and wherever a staff skill appears in a choice, show the skill's word beside the icon the first time on that screen. | Artist (icon), Moments (label) |
| 4 | "Graphics should be a little more detailed. Maybe that means more dense pixel graphics." | Art checkpoint first: the Artist makes a side-by-side SAMPLE (current vs denser pixels at the same on-screen size). The Lead shows it to the owner. No full redraw until he picks. | Artist (sample) |
| 5 | "Random events happen a little too often, it feels almost too chaotic, and they are always positive. Most of the time they should be positive but sometimes negative." | Fewer happenings (roughly one every 10 to 14 weeks instead of 7) and about one in three is a mild setback. Setbacks stay mild: they slow you, never undo progress, never cost an animal, staff or building. | Rules |
| 6 | Trader "comes off a little stereotypical, so redo him." | New trader design: a friendly travelling animal dealer with no ethnic or national costume cues (for example a safari-style work shirt, a cap, a clipboard, a small animal carrier). | Artist |
| 7 | "I love the mystery crate but maybe there should be rarity crates and each time he comes he only has 5 crates of one random rarity, and after you open it it gives you the option to pick between selling and keeping and where to keep." | Each trader visit brings 5 crates of ONE random rarity (price and odds by rarity, rarer = rarer visit). Opening one reveals the animal, then: Sell (shows the price) or Keep, then pick which habitat (or the holding pen). | Rules, Moments, Menus |
| 8 | "The trader also almost fits all 4 animals and the crate all on one screen without scrolling so see if you can condense a little to avoid a scroll." | Trader screen fits with no scrolling at 390 x 844 and on the iPhone 18 Pro: compact rows. | Moments, Menus (Market tab) |
| 9 | "The screen on my iPhone 18 Pro it's not taking advantage of the top and bottom of the screen; it needs to take advantage of all the space." | In his home-screen app the game ends well above the bottom of the screen (a dark band under the news line, then a second band). Likely cause: the layout follows `visualViewport.height`, which on iOS home-screen apps can be shorter than the screen. Fix, and add a "Screen check" readout under System (with dev tools on) that shows the real numbers so he can send a screenshot. | Shell |
| 10 | "The pens should start at 4 spaces and upgrade at 2 spaces an upgrade." | Lead reading: "pens" means habitats ("Savanna 1 5/5"). Every habitat holds 4 at level 1 and +2 per upgrade level, whatever its shape. The holding pen is unchanged. Rebalance with the bot. | Rules |
| 11 | "Staffing: you should send out a hire action and it should take time to get back applicants." | Advertising becomes a timed job (a couple of weeks; better tiers may take longer). Applicants arrive later as a pop-up with the reveal, then the "Who turned up" list. | Rules, Menus, Moments |
| 12 | "When an expedition comes back the animal has an overlapping grade; it shouldn't overlap but I do want the grade there. It shouldn't automatically be put in a pen; it should give the option of selling and then if you keep you should pick where it goes." | Reveal card: grade badge beside the animal, never on top (screenshot 3). After each found animal: Sell (price shown) or Keep, then choose the habitat (best fits first; holding pen as the last choice). Same flow for crates. Births still go to their parents' habitat. | Moments, Rules |
| 13 | "The animals screen is just too much scrolling... They should also be ordered by animal type in each pen." | Animals screen: compact rows (portrait, sex, three stat numbers with icons, grade) about half the current height, sorted by species within each habitat, most of a typical zoo visible without scrolling. The stat key line can go once rows are self-explanatory. | Menus |
